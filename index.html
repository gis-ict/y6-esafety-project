How to read the blocks

🟧 The mouth of a C-block is the space inside it. Blocks in the mouth are inside the loop or the if.

⬇ Blocks under the bottom arm are outside. They run after the loop or the if.

👀 Look for the small labels: inside forever, inside if, inside else.

ບລັອກທີ່ຢູ່ໃນປາກຂອງບລັອກຮູບຕົວ C ແມ່ນຢູ່ "ພາຍໃນ" loop ຫຼື if. ບລັອກທີ່ຢູ່ລຸ່ມແຂນລຸ່ມ ແມ່ນເຮັດວຽກ "ຫຼັງຈາກ".

Script 1: Start the game

📝 Read it in words
What it does
Sets up the game: score 0, lives 3, timer 120. Asks the player's name and uses it.
Then it runs each question in order with broadcast and wait. If the player gets to the end, it broadcasts win.
INPUTVARIABLE
ຕັ້ງຄ່າເລີ່ມຕົ້ນ ແລະ ຖາມຊື່ຜູ້ຫຼິ້ນ.
Script 2: One question
📝 Read it in words
What it does
Asks one question. If the answer is right, score goes up. Else the player loses a life and Giga gives a safety tip.
Then it checks: no lives left, or no time left? Then the player loses.
Make the next question: right-click the hat block → Duplicate, then change q1 to q2, the question, the answer and the tip.
INPUTBRANCHVARIABLE
ຖາມ 1 ຄຳຖາມ. ຖືກ: score +1. ຜິດ: lives -1 ແລະ ໃຫ້ຄຳແນະນຳ.
Script 3: The timer
📝 Read it in words
What it does
Counts down 1 every second. This keeps the game under 2 minutes.
LOOPVARIABLE
ນັບຖອຍຫຼັງທຸກໆ 1 ວິນາທີ.
Script 4: WIN
📝 Read it in words
What it does
The player answered every question. Stop other scripts stops the timer.
5 out of 5: party backdrop and Giga dances (a repeat loop). Less than 5: Safe Helper.
WINBRANCHLOOP
ຊະນະ: ຕອບຄົບທຸກຂໍ້.
Script 5: LOSE
📝 Read it in words
What it does
No lives or no time left. The game stops, says why the player lost, and reminds them of a safety rule.
LOSEBRANCH
ແພ້: ໝົດຊີວິດ ຫຼື ໝົດເວລາ.

  
New block: broadcast. It sends a message. Any script that starts with when I receive that message will run. Find it in Events (yellow). Broadcast and wait waits until that script is finished.
broadcast = ສົ່ງຂໍ້ຄວາມ ໃຫ້ script ອື່ນເລີ່ມເຮັດວຽກ.

The 12 e-safety rules
    
  
  1. Think before you post. Online can stay online.
  2. Deleted is not gone. Friends can screenshot it.
    3. 
  
  3. Keep info private. Address, school, phone, birthday.
  4. Ask before you post a friend's photo.
    5. 
  
  5. Use two locks (MFA). A code on a grown-up's phone.
  6. Know, have, are. Password, phone code, fingerprint.
    7. 
  
  7. Long and random wins. pizza-moon-rocket.
  8. Never share passwords or login codes.
    9. 
  
  9. Free prize? Stop! Close pop-ups that want passwords.
  10. Don't click strange links.
    11. 
  
  11. Strangers stay strangers in game chat.
  12. Tell a trusted adult if something feels wrong.

👣 Digital footprint and privacy
Sam deletes a silly photo. Is every copy gone?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: Friends can screenshot and share it.
Should you post your home address online?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: Keep your address private.
A photo shows your school uniform. Is it safe to post for everyone?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: Strangers can find your school.
Should you ask a friend before you post their photo?
Type: y or n · Right answer: y · Tip if wrong: Their photo is their footprint too.
Which is safe to share? a = your favourite colour  b = your phone number
Type: a or b · Right answer: a · Tip if wrong: Phone numbers are private.
Are your searches part of your digital footprint?
Type: y or n · Right answer: y · Tip if wrong: Everything you do online leaves a footprint.
🔐 Locks and passwords
An app sends a code to your grown-up's phone when you log in. Is that a good extra lock?
Type: y or n · Right answer: y · Tip if wrong: Two locks (MFA) are safer than one.
A fingerprint is a lock you... k = KNOW  h = HAVE  a = ARE
Type: k, h or a · Right answer: a · Tip if wrong: A fingerprint is part of you.
A password is a lock you... k = KNOW  h = HAVE  a = ARE
Type: k, h or a · Right answer: k · Tip if wrong: A password is in your head.
A phone code is a lock you... k = KNOW  h = HAVE  a = ARE
Type: k, h or a · Right answer: h · Tip if wrong: You hold the phone in your hand.
Which password is stronger? a = 123456  b = pizza-moon-rocket
Type: a or b · Right answer: b · Tip if wrong: Long and random is strong.
Your best friend asks for your password. Should you tell them?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: Only you and your grown-ups know it.
Is your pet's name a good password?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: People can guess it from your posts.
🚨 Tricks and strangers
A pop-up says: type your password to win a prize! Should you type it?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: Prizes never need passwords. Close it.
Someone you don't know sends a link: "Is this you??" Should you click it?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: Don't click. Tell a grown-up.
A player in a game chat asks which school you go to. Should you tell them?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: Strangers stay strangers.
Something online makes you feel scared. What should you do? a = keep it secret  b = tell a grown-up
Type: a or b · Right answer: b · Tip if wrong: Always tell a trusted adult.
A free torch app wants your contacts. Should you tap Allow?
Type: y or n · Right answer: n · Tip if wrong: A torch does not need your contacts.
